Guide To Your Hikes



Hiking is a recreational outdoor activity involving long, vigorous walks on natural trails, footpaths, or rugged terrain like mountains and forests. It is a sport enjoyable by people who prefer moderate exercise while being exposed to the beauty of nature.



Required Equipment
  • Footwear (suitable for hiking)
  • Trekking Poles
  • Clothing (Layered)
  • Food and Water
  • Navigation
  • Light Source
  • Emergency Items/First Aid Kit
Important Reminders
  • Check the Weather
  • Know your Limits
  • Pack the Applicable Essentials
  • Hydrate and Fuel Regularly
  • Follow Trail Etiquette
Footwork and Body Mechanics
  • Use Uphill and Downhill Tecnique
  • Choose a Comfortable and Efficient Pacing
  • Utilize Trekking Poles
